# Footy Draw

Fixtures you can subscribe to: the A-League, Premier League, Scottish Premiership,
European and Asian club competitions, national teams, NRL and AFL. Every match is in your
calendar with its kick-off time in your own timezone, and it stays up to date: when a
match moves for TV or gets postponed, your calendar changes too. Matches drop off a week
after they've been played.

**Pick your teams and competitions at [footydraw.com](https://footydraw.com)** and get
them all in one calendar. A club comes with every competition it plays in: pick Arsenal
and you get the Premier League, the cups and Europe.

## Subscribing

Use the buttons on [footydraw.com](https://footydraw.com), or copy a URL and add it to
your calendar app as a **subscription**
(often called *add by URL*). Don't download and import the file. An imported file is
a one-off copy that never updates.

- **Google Calendar**: on the web, go to *Other calendars* → **+** → *From URL*,
  paste the URL and click *Add calendar*. It then appears on your phone too.
- **iPhone / iPad**: *Settings* → *Apps* → *Calendar* → *Calendar Accounts* →
  *Add Account* → *Other* → *Add Subscribed Calendar*, and paste the URL.
- **Mac**: in Calendar, *File* → *New Calendar Subscription*, paste the URL, and set
  *Auto-refresh* to every hour or every day.
- **Outlook**: *Add calendar* → *Subscribe from web*, and paste the URL.

To stop, remove or unsubscribe from the calendar in your app.

## What's in each match

- Title with the teams, e.g. `⚽ Sydney FC v Western Sydney Wanderers`. In a calendar
  that mixes competitions, the competition comes first: `⚽ EPL · Arsenal v Chelsea`.
- The venue as the location, plus the competition and round.
- Postponed or cancelled matches are marked `[Postponed]` or `[Cancelled]`.
- If a kick-off time hasn't been announced yet, the match shows as an all-day event
  on its date until it is.

## Good to know

- The calendars are rebuilt every 6 hours, but each app decides how often it checks
  for changes. Google can take up to a day, so a late time change may not show up
  straight away. Apple lets you choose the refresh interval.
- Between seasons a calendar is empty until the new draw is released (the NRL and AFL
  draws usually come out in late October or November), and tournaments are empty
  between editions.
